2. Where they are

The US is the largest market, then India and the UK. In every large market, about two thirds of users shop or book something through Fo, and roughly a quarter to a third shop for goods.

3. Kinds of shopping missions

Most shopping starts with finding and comparing, and those tasks almost always succeed. The harder missions are the transactional ones: booking, buying, cancelling and disputes. Those are also where phone calls come in.

4. How complex it gets

The model rated each task 1 to 3. Complex tasks involve several merchants, logins, payments, negotiation or disputes. They succeed less often, and most of them need a phone call.

How this was measured
  • Tasks: every request a user gave Fo, as recorded by Fo's own conversation grader. Each task was labelled by an AI model (Gemini 3.5 Flash) with category, mission, complexity, whether a phone call was involved, and the merchant. Spot checks agreed with the labels.
  • “Shopping” covers goods, food, travel, tickets, services, subscriptions and bills. “Retail” is physical goods plus restaurant food delivery.
  • Country comes from the user's phone number, or their timezone when there is no phone.
  • Users are the most reliable measure. One mission often splits into several tasks, and call counts include redials.
  • Purchases: one purchase per user, merchant and conversation. “Completed” means the card charge cleared.
